Water Extraction

Residential Water Extraction Services

A broken pipe, a leaking appliance, or a storm can flood a house fast, and extraction by professionals remains the safest answer and the fastest one. From a small leak to a fully flooded floor, Wilson Restoration removes water from homes across Wilson. On each job we assess the damage, pump out the water with industrial equipment, map the moisture, protect your belongings, and document everything for your insurer.

Your belongings, the structure, and your family's health are all in danger once water pools inside. It takes only hours for drywall to swell, hardwood to warp, and electrical components to start corroding. If left untreated for 24 to 48 hours, mold growth becomes almost inevitable. Our advice is simple: pick up the phone as soon as you find water pooling indoors, no matter how little there is. The sooner we arrive, the sooner we can measure what is wet and get extraction going, ahead of any secondary damage.

Hidden leaks that have dripped for days or weeks are part of our work too. Long before anything shows on the surface, a slow drip under flooring or inside a wall can do serious harm to the structure. Our technicians use thermal imaging cameras and professional moisture meters to locate hidden moisture pockets and ensure complete drying.

Structural Drying

Structural Drying With Daily Moisture Checks

After water gets in, nothing matters more for the long-term health of your house than drying the structure all the way through. Proper drying eliminates hidden moisture from walls, subfloors, and cavities, prevents mold growth, and restores your home's structural integrity. Wilson Restoration dries every home to the IICRC S500 standard, checking and recording moisture levels each day.

Our residential drying setup covers high-velocity air movers, commercial dehumidifiers, specialty systems made for wall cavities and hardwood floors, plus thermal imaging to keep verifying moisture as we go. No two houses dry the same way, so we build the plan around your layout, your building materials, and how bad the damage is.

Professional drying goes much further than most people expect. The puddles may be gone, but dampness left inside framing, beneath floors, and within walls can feed mold and rot wood for weeks afterward. We monitor moisture levels daily and don't consider the job complete until every reading reaches pre-loss standards. Expect 3 to 5 days for a typical house, with the exact time set by how far the damage goes.

Stopping Mold

Home Mold Remediation and Prevention

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, spreading rapidly through your home and creating serious health risks for your family. If mold is already growing, Wilson Restoration can remediate it as a standalone service, and prevention is built into each restoration project we do. For mold work, our IICRC-certified team sticks to the EPA guidance and the IICRC S520 standard so the job is both safe and complete.

We recommend mold prevention treatment as part of any water damage restoration project. Once mold has settled in, removing it is harder and more expensive than using an antimicrobial treatment during drying. However, if you already have visible mold or suspect hidden mold from a previous water event, we provide comprehensive testing, containment, removal, and post-remediation verification to ensure your home is safe and healthy.

Insurance Support

Help With Your Wilson Water Damage Claim

Navigating a water damage insurance claim can be overwhelming, especially while your home is disrupted. Wilson Restoration works directly with all major insurance carriers to streamline the claims process for you. Photos, readings from our moisture meters, the materials that were affected, and the work we perform all go into a complete report that satisfies what insurance companies ask for.

Having worked through claims with thousands of homeowners in Wilson, we know what an adjuster looks for before approving coverage. Because we document thoroughly and talk to your insurer directly, claims tend to be processed sooner and to account for all of the damage. Ask us anything about your policy along the way, and count on us to go over it with you and represent your interests until the work is finished.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage such as burst pipes, appliance failures, and storm-related intrusion. Knowing what your policy includes matters, and we will help you sort that out and claim everything you are entitled to. A small investment in professional documentation often results in significantly higher insurance payouts compared to homeowners who file claims without professional support.

Sewer Backups

Safe Sewage Cleanup in Your Home

Few kinds of water damage are as dangerous to a household as a sewage backup. The health risks are serious because Category 3 water, also called black water, carries bacteria and viruses along with other pathogens. Wilson Restoration technicians are specially trained and equipped to handle sewage cleanup safely, following strict IICRC protocols for contaminated water restoration.

Our sewage cleanup process includes containment of the affected area, safe extraction of contaminated water, removal of unsalvageable materials, thorough antimicrobial treatment, complete structural drying, and post-restoration testing to verify your home is safe. One team does the whole job so that your family is back in a clean and healthy house without delay.

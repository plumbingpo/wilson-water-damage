Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Wilson or the surrounding area, Wilson Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Wilson

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
